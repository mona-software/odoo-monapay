MONA Pay for Odoo eCommerce

Dynamic VietQR with automatic bank-transfer confirmation

Let customers scan an exact-amount VietQR code at checkout. A signed MONA Pay webhook marks the Odoo payment transaction as paid and lets Odoo confirm the sales order.

Funds go directly to your bank account. MONA Pay does not collect or hold funds.

Secure and idempotent

  • HMAC-SHA256 verification on the unmodified webhook body.
  • Five-minute replay protection and duplicate transaction detection.
  • Exact order-content matching with virtual-account fallback.
  • Underpayments never mark an order as paid.

Supports Odoo 17/18. See monapay.vn and documentation.
